About Boho Boho

A relaxed space where good food meets good company

Boho Boho is a bistro and cafe tucked into the heart of Koregaon Park — a neighbourhood made for unhurried afternoons, creative conversations and discovering new favourites one plate at a time.

The menu brings together fresh, globally-inspired dishes alongside specialty coffee — from warming breakfasts and vibrant salads to hearty pasta and pizzas. Come in for one thing, stay for everything else.
