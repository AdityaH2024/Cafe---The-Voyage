Our Story

A café built on good vibes and better food

Ember & Bean started as a single roastery counter with three tables, built on the idea that a genuinely good cup of coffee deserves an equally thoughtful plate of food alongside it.

Today the menu spans specialty coffee, hearty brunch, wood-fired pizza and Asian-inspired bowls — all served in a space designed for slow mornings, working afternoons and long dinners with people who matter.
